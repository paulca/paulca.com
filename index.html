Mauritania

The Islamic Republic of Mauritania is a country in Northwest Africa, largely defined by its Sahara Desert landscape. It gained independence from France on November 28, 1960.

Capital City: Nouakchott

Nouakchott is located on the Atlantic coast at the mouth of the Oued Rouïki river. It is known for having no harbor and sitting behind a beach fringed by sand dunes.

Key Facts

  • Official Religion: Islam (80-85% of the population).
  • Languages: Arabic (official) and French.
  • Currency: Ouguiya (MRU).
